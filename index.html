How Aircraft.co.uk Works

A UK-first HR Setup-as-a-Service platform transforming first-hire complexity into structured, compliant infrastructure in minutes.

01

Intent Intake

5–8 guided setup questions.

Founder answers role title, employment type, salary, location, start date and sector. The system maps inputs to UK PAYE, NIC, GDPR, pension and right-to-work requirements.
02

One-Click Deployment

Full HR Pack generated instantly.

Aircraft.co.uk generates compliant contracts, core HR policies, payroll readiness setup, onboarding workflow and a compliance checklist — assembled into a structured Pack Manifest.
03

Contract & Policy Engine

Legally aligned document generation.

Employment contracts, handbook sections, grievance, sickness, data handling and holiday policies are generated using rule-based validation aligned with UK statutory minima.
04

Embedded Payroll Setup

PAYE, NIC & pension configuration.

Built-in payroll engine calculates PAYE, NIC and pension assessments. RTI preparation steps are configured and ready for HMRC submission.
05

Compliance Wizard

Right-to-work & GDPR tracking.

Interactive compliance checks guide founders through right-to-work verification, pension duties, GDPR basics, IR35 indicators and structured evidence storage.
06

Onboarding Workflow

Task automation for new hires.

Automated onboarding tasks include contract e-sign, handbook acknowledgment, IT setup reminders, probation scheduling and compliance checkpoints.
07

Risk & Audit Engine

IR35 & payroll anomaly detection.

Built-in heuristics detect misclassification risk, payroll anomalies and missing compliance evidence — supported by audit logs and version history tracking.
08

Live Dashboard

Single source of HR truth.

Founders monitor contracts, payroll status, compliance alerts and onboarding progress from one centralized operational dashboard.
09

Self-Healing Documents

Auto-updated legal framework.

When regulatory thresholds change, the system maps affected clauses, proposes redlines with citations, and preserves full version history with audit traceability.
